## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Brazilian santinho, the devotional holy card people keep in wallets, whose figure is a Dragão de Luz in a golden resplendor. It refuses the category default of a lilac galaxy hero over a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep emerald owns the page ground (user-pinned, replacing the first ultramarine build); resplendor gold carries rays, filetes, display type and the primary action; ivory appears as the card stock with its die-cut lace (renda) edge and as the one light section (Como funciona). Bodoni Moda for display and the prayer text, the system UI face (SF Pro on Apple devices) for reading and controls. Double gold filetes, scalloped lace borders, sun-and-star ornaments (no crosses).

STORY: The visitor recognises a santinho, learns who Anelise is and what a session proposes, reads the back of the card like a prayer (manifesto), browses the sessions as a small set of cards by intention, sees how it works and the honest disclaimer, and books on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Emerald field under a slowly turning gold resplendor. Centered: "Luz que se move em você.", the lead and a working booking strip (sessão select + "Agendar pelo WhatsApp" gold pill). The santinho (lace edge, Dragão de Luz plate, "Dragão de Luz" in the lower cartouche) peeks from the bottom edge. Glass wordmark nav on top.

FORM: assigned candidate 3 of 7 (Santinho de Luz) after one factual re-roll (candidate 7, apothecary, implied prescribed remedies against PRODUCT.md's no-cure constraint). seed: none (unavailable)
Signature interaction: the hero is a pinned 240svh scene. Scrolling blurs the headline away, raises the santinho to the centre and turns it over to its back, the manifesto set as a prayer. The card can also be dragged 1:1 or flipped by button, settling on a spring that keeps the finger's velocity. Elsewhere, scroll-driven CSS: rays turn, the garland unrolls, the Contabilidade Cósmica sentence lights word by word, steps rise, headings settle out of blur, the session cards fan out. Reduced motion shows front and back side by side, still.
