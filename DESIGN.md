# Design System: Anelise Werle Camillo, Terapeuta Quântica

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Santinho de Luz"**

The whole site is a Brazilian santinho, the devotional holy card kept in a wallet, laid on a deep emerald ground and lit by gold. The ground is the page; the one object you hold up close is the hero santinho: cream card stock inside a die-cut lace edge, a Dragão de Luz on the front, a short prayer on the back. Gold is the resplendor: the sunburst rays behind the card and the portrait, the filetes around paper, the one pill-shaped action. The only fully light section, "Como funciona", is a printed ivory page. Ornaments are suns and four-point stars, never crosses.

The materials and motion are Apple-grade. The header is a sheet of frosted glass that densifies once the page leaves the top. The opening is a pinned scene: the stage stays put for 240svh of scroll while the title dissolves, the santinho rises to centre and turns over. The card can also be grabbed and flipped by hand, tracking the finger 1:1 and settling on a spring. Everything else is scroll-driven CSS: rays turn, the garland unrolls, the manifesto lights word by word, steps rise, headings settle out of a blur, the session cards fan out of a single pile. Density is low and ceremonial; Didone headings carry the devotion, the system UI face carries everything read.

The site explicitly refuses the category default of a lilac galaxy hero over a row of identical service cards.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Emerald ground in three depths (`esm-950`, `esm-900`, `esm-800`); ivory only on card stock and the "Como funciona" page.
- Gold for rays, filetes, headings on emerald and the single primary action.
- Bodoni Moda for display, headings, names and the prayer; the system UI stack (SF Pro Text on Apple devices) for everything read and every control.
- Ornament vocabulary: four-point star, eight-ray sun, scalloped lace, double filete, repeating-conic sunburst.
- Motion: a pinned, scroll-scrubbed hero scene with a spring-driven card flip; scroll-linked CSS animations elsewhere; two curves only.

## Colors

Emerald and gold are the house colours (user-pinned brand commitment); ivory is the paper; nothing else is an ink.

### Primary
- **Deep Emerald** (`esm-900`): the page ground, the hero scene, sessions and Dragões sections, `theme-color`.
- **Night Emerald** (`esm-950`): the darker band for "Sobre", contact and footer; the glass header tint; the opaque header under reduced transparency; the scrollbar track.
- **Lit Emerald** (`esm-800`): the portrait medallion fill. Same value as `tinta`.
- **Engraved Emerald** (`esm-700`): emerald read on paper: card links, the prayer's fading rules and signature.
- **Mint Glint** (`esm-300`): the small stars in the Dragões element list only.

### Secondary
- **Resplendor Gold** (`ouro`): the gold pill and button mid-stop, select chevron and border, FAQ chevrons, the wordmark subtitle, the contact sun, frame lines at 20-50% alpha (`filete`, `filete-campo`, `filete-rodape`, `raio-medalhao`).
- **Pale Gold** (`ouro-claro`): every heading on emerald, the hero title, focus rings, the skip link, the "Virar o santinho" control, the hero rays (`raio`, 30%), the mobile menu links.
- **Engraver's Gold** (`ouro-escuro`): gold on paper: the card stock hairline (`filete-papel`, `filete-papel-2`), the cartouche double border, the sun on the card back, scrollbar thumbs.
- **Text Gold** (`ouro-texto`): gold that must read on ivory: the step numerals, placeholder marks on paper.
- **Gilt highlight and base** (`ouro-reflexo`, `ouro-base`): only as the top and bottom stops of the gilt gradient on the gold button and floating button.

### Neutral
- **Ivory** (`marfim`): card stock, the cartouche, the "Como funciona" page, select options.
- **Shaded Ivory** (`marfim-2`): the plate behind card art while it loads.
- **Step Paper** (`marfim-passo`): the step cards on the ivory page, edged by `borda-passo`.
- **Milk** (`texto`): body text, wordmark and FAQ questions on emerald.
- **Sage Milk** (`texto-2`): secondary copy on emerald: lead, section intros, nav links at rest, notes, answers, footer.
- **Ink** (`tinta`): headings and text on paper; text on gold; selection text; focus ring on paper.
- **Soft Ink** (`tinta-2`): body copy on paper.

### Material roles
- **Glass** (`vidro` at rest, `vidro-denso` once scrolled, `vidro-borda` for the fading under-edge): the header tint under the blur. **Menu sheet** (`folha-menu`): the mobile menu's near-opaque emerald.
- **Field** (`campo`, `campo-hover`): the translucent white wash inside the select.
- **Light and shade** (`brilho`, `sombra`, `sombra-funda`, `sombra-renda`, `poco-icone`): the inset top highlight, ambient drop shadows, the lace lift and the icon well inside the gold button.
- **Placeholder wash** (`marca-ph`, `marca-ph-papel`): the fill behind unconfirmed content.
- **Mask channels** (`mascara`, `mascara-meia`): opaque and half stops inside the radial masks that fade the rays. Never painted as colour.

### Named Rules
**The Emerald Ground Rule.** The page ground is always emerald. Never swap it for another hue; ivory appears only as card stock or the single printed "Como funciona" page.

**The Inked Gold Rule.** Bright gold (`ouro`, `ouro-claro`) belongs to emerald; on ivory, gold drops to `ouro-escuro` for lines and `ouro-texto` for text, and links drop to `esm-700`. Swap the ink, never lower the contrast.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bodoni Moda, variable 400-900, self-hosted woff2 with `font-display: swap` (with Didot, Bodoni 72, Georgia)
**Body / UI Font:** the system UI stack (`system-ui, -apple-system, "SF Pro Text", "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`)

**Character:** A high-contrast engraved Didone, mixed case and tightly tracked, carries the devotional voice; the platform's own text face carries reading and controls so the page feels native on the phone it is read on.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Bodoni 500, fluid 2.9-6rem, 1.02, -0.025em, optical sizing auto): the hero title only, in `ouro-claro`, two lines that stack on desktop and run inline under 560px.
- **Headline** (Bodoni 500, fluid 2.2-4.5rem, 1.04): every section `h2`, `ouro-claro` on emerald, `tinta` on the ivory page; the contact heading steps up to `headline-contato`.
- **Frase** (Bodoni, fluid 1.9-3.6rem, 1.15): the "Contabilidade Cósmica" manifesto, max 24em, in `texto`.
- **Title** (Bodoni 500, 1.45rem on session cards, 1.35rem on steps): names printed on paper.
- **Numeral** (Bodoni 3rem, `ouro-texto`): roman step numerals I-IV.
- **Wordmark** (Bodoni 1.05rem, header; 1.15rem footer) over a `micro` UI subtitle in `ouro`.
- **Oração / Cartela / Assinatura** (Bodoni): the prayer on the card back (max 16em), the cartouche name plate, the uppercase 0.08em signature.
- **Lead / Intro** (UI 400, fluid up to 1.3rem / 1.25rem): hero paragraph and section intros, max 36-38rem.
- **Body** (UI 400, 1.0625rem, 1.55, -0.01em): all running text.
- **Label** (UI 650, 1rem): the gold button; FAQ questions use `question` at 600.
- **Small / Nav / Note / Micro** (UI 0.9375 / 0.875 / 0.8125 / 0.75rem): controls, nav links and pill, notes and footer, the wordmark subtitle.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Bodoni for anything that is a name, a heading or a devotion; the system UI face for anything read or pressed. No third family.

**The Mixed Case Rule.** Bodoni is set in mixed case with negative tracking; uppercase only for the prayer signature.

## Layout

A single centred column with ceremony. The gutter is `spacing.gutter`; most sections breathe on `spacing.section` vertical padding. Text measures cap at 34-46rem; the card fan and step grid at 74rem; the about split at 72rem; FAQ at 52rem.

- **Header:** fixed, `spacing.topo` tall, wordmark left, five text links, gold pill right. Anchors land with `scroll-padding-top` of header + 1.5rem.
- **Hero scene:** a 240svh section holding a sticky 100svh stage. Text sits top-centre; the santinho (`min(20rem, 62vw, 56svh at 1100/1640)`) starts low and rises to centre. Rays are a centred conic burst at `max(70rem, 150vw)`.
- **Sessions:** three lace cards fanned like a hand (outer ±3deg and dropped 1.6rem, centre raised); hover or focus straightens and lifts.
- **About:** 0.8fr / 1.2fr split, oval medallion left, then the manifesto centred below.
- **Como funciona:** four step cards in a grid; two columns at 960px, one at 560px.
- **Breakpoints:** 960px (steps to two columns); 860px (nav becomes a clip-path sheet, booking stacks to `min(22rem, 100%)`, the fan becomes a horizontal scroll-snap rail of `min(19rem, 80vw)` cards, about stacks); 560px (one-column steps, wordmark subtitle hidden).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a hybrid of Apple materials and lifted paper. The header is translucent glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)` over `vidro`, densifying to `vidro-denso` with a 1rem fading under-edge once the page scrolls past 24px). Paper lifts off the emerald with soft ambient drop shadows; gold objects carry an inset top highlight. No hard offset shadows, no glows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lace lift** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 30px 40px rgba(0, 8, 5, .55))`): the santinho faces and session cards, as drop-shadow so the scallops cast.
- **Gilt lift** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 26px -14px rgba(0,0,0,.7), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.5)`; `0 14px 30px -12px rgba(0,0,0,.8)` on the floating button): gold buttons.
- **Medallion** (`0 0 0 6px esm-950, 0 0 0 7px gold at 50%, 0 30px 60px -24px rgba(0,0,0,.8)`): the portrait oval, a ring with a deep ambient fall.
- **Double filete** (`inset 0 0 0 3px marfim, inset 0 0 0 4px filete-papel-2` inside a 1px `filete-papel` border): card stock.

### Named Rules
**The Glass Fallback Rule.** Every translucent surface has an opaque answer: under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` the header becomes solid `esm-950` with no blur.

## Shapes

Card stock is square-cornered inside the scalloped, pin-pricked lace edge (`assets/renda.svg` as `border-image`, slice 16, `fill round`, 20px on the santinho, 16px on cards); that lace is the signature silhouette. Controls are pills (`rounded.pill`): the header pill, the select, the gold button. Circles (`rounded.round`) hold the icon well, the floating button and the oval medallion. The step cards on the ivory page are softly rounded (`rounded.passo`). Focus rings and placeholder marks round to `rounded.foco`. The sunburst is a `repeating-conic-gradient` of hairline gold rays faded by a radial mask, behind the hero card and the medallion. The cartouche uses a `3px double` gold border.

## Components

### Buttons
Polished gilt, pressed like glass.
- **Gold button:** pill, 3.1rem high, vertical gradient `ouro-reflexo` to `ouro` (60%) to `ouro-base`, `tinta` label at 650, label left and a 2.3rem round icon well (`poco-icone`) with the WhatsApp mark right. Large variant 3.6rem / 1.125rem for contact.
- **Header pill:** flat `ouro`, 2rem, 0.875rem at 600.
- **Press / Hover:** every pressable scales to 0.97 on `:active` (160ms, ease-out); hover (fine pointers only) brightens 1.05-1.06 and nudges the icon 2px right. On submit the label blurs out, swaps to "Abrindo o WhatsApp…" and swaps back after 2.6s.
- **Focus:** global 2px `ouro-claro` outline, 3px offset, `rounded.foco`; `tinta` on paper.
- **Text action:** "Virar o santinho" in `ouro-claro` with a star that rotates 180deg when the back faces; card links in `esm-700`, underlined on hover with a 3px arrow nudge.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Select:** pill, 3.1rem, 1px `filete-campo` border over `campo`, CSS chevron in `ouro`. Hover: border `ouro-claro`, fill `campo-hover`. Options render `tinta` on `marfim`.

### Navigation
Glass header. Links in `texto-2`, hovering to `texto`. Below 860px a two-line icon button crosses to an X; the menu is a full-screen `folha-menu` sheet revealed by `clip-path: circle()` grown from the button (opens 0.5s ease-out, closes 0.3s ease-in-out), links in Bodoni `ouro-claro` rising 1rem in a 40ms stagger. Escape, link tap and focus leaving the header close it.

### Cards / Containers
- **Session card:** lace edge, `marfim` with double filete, 4:3 art plate, Bodoni title, `tinta-2` copy, format line, `esm-700` link. Lace lift.
- **Step card:** `marfim-passo`, 1px `borda-passo`, `rounded.passo`, roman numeral in `ouro-texto`.

### Santinho (signature)
A 1100:1640 lace card with two faces. Front: Dragão de Luz art with the cartouche "Dragão de Luz". Back: a sun in `ouro-escuro`, the prayer between two fading `esm-700` rules, uppercase signature. Scroll drives it: over the first 28% of the scene the text fades, lifts 6svh, shrinks 4% and blurs 6px; over 34% the card rises from 76% of the viewport to centre and grows from 0.86 to 1; from 40% to 78% it turns from -12deg to 180deg (rotateX 3deg, perspective 1600px), all eased with an ease-out cubic. The hand adds its own angle: drag tracks 1:1 after an 8px slop, release projects momentum (deceleration 0.995 over the last 100ms) to pick a face and hands the finger's velocity to a spring (response 0.4s, damping 0.8); the button and a plain tap use a critically damped spring (response 0.45s, damping 1). Held, the card scales to 0.985.

### FAQ
Native `details` between `filete` hairlines; question in `question` weight 600 `texto`, a gold chevron rotating from 45deg to 225deg; the answer height animates through `::details-content` (0.3s ease-out, `interpolate-size: allow-keywords`).

### Floating WhatsApp
A 3.5rem gilt circle bottom-right (safe-area aware); hidden and inert while the hero booking is in view, then rises 1rem and fades in (0.25s ease-out).

### Placeholder mark
Unconfirmed content stays visibly marked: `ouro-claro` text on `marca-ph`, 1px dashed outline (`ouro-texto` on `marca-ph-papel` on paper). A content-state marker, removed when the fact is confirmed.

### Motion system
Two curves: ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for everything that arrives or responds, ease-in-out `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)` only for the menu closing. Opening entry: title lines, lead and booking materialise from 8px blur and 0.5em down over 900ms, staggered 80ms; rays fade in over 1600ms. Behind `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`: rays rotate 40deg with root scroll; the medallion rays rotate with its view; the garland unrolls from the centre; the manifesto lights word by word from 20% opacity; steps rise 2.5rem staggered; section headings settle from 6px blur; on desktop the three cards fan out of one pile; on mobile the centred card in the rail scales to 1 while neighbours sit at 0.92 / 60%. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the scene unpins, both santinho faces sit side by side, flip controls hide, and nothing is left hidden.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep emerald the ground and gold the light; ivory only as lace-edged card stock or the single printed page.
- **Do** keep gold the only action colour: one gold pill or button per view, plus the floating button once the hero booking scrolls away.
- **Do** set names, headings and devotions in Bodoni Moda and everything read or pressed in the system UI stack.
- **Do** use the four-point star, the sun, the lace edge and the conic sunburst as the ornament set.
- **Do** ease on `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`, scale pressables to 0.97, and gate hover effects to `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Do** put scroll-linked effects behind `@supports (animation-timeline: ...)` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and give every translucent surface a reduced-transparency fallback.
- **Do** swap to `ouro-escuro`, `ouro-texto` and `esm-700` when gold or emerald sits on ivory.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use crosses or confessional iconography; the ornaments are suns and stars.
- **Don't** fall back to the category default of a lilac galaxy hero over a row of identical service cards.
- **Don't** swap the emerald ground for another hue or add a third accent ink.
- **Don't** add a third type family or set Bodoni body copy.
- **Don't** use hard offset shadows or glows; depth is glass and lifted paper.
- **Don't** animate on a timer what the scroll or the hand can drive; the rays never spin on their own.
