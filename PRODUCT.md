# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user's choice). No build step; deployable to GitHub Pages, Netlify or Hostinger.

## Users

People in Brazil (Portuguese-speaking) looking for an integrative / energy therapist: usually adults going through a hard phase (stress, blocks, grief, prosperity or relationship worries) who already believe in or are curious about energy work, beings of light and the law of attraction. They arrive mostly from Instagram on a phone, decide on feeling and trust, and act by sending a WhatsApp message.

## Product Purpose

The personal practice site of **Anelise Werle Camillo, Terapeuta Quântica**. Its only job is to get visitors to book a session (atendimento). Success = a visitor understands what a session with her is, trusts her, and taps "Agendar pelo WhatsApp". Courses are out of scope for this site (user decision).

## Positioning

Her own public vocabulary (Instagram posts signed "Anelise Werle Camillo Terapeuta Quântica"): Dragões de Luz as spiritual guides, seres de luz, mestres ascensos, seres solares, elementos da natureza, energia/física quântica, vibração, lei da atração, lei do retorno, dar e receber, Mesa Quântica, "Contabilidade Cósmica". The Dragões de Luz lineage plus the Mesa Quântica is what a generic "terapeuta holística" page could not copy.

## Operating Context

- Sessions are typically online (video call or at a distance, often with an audio/written report afterwards) or in person; format per modality is unconfirmed and must be confirmed by the client.
- Booking happens on WhatsApp; there is no booking system.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed (public trace): professional name and title; Dragões de Luz; Mesa Quântica (hashtag #mesaquantica); quantum/energy and law-of-attraction framing.
- Unconfirmed, must stay marked as placeholders: photo, WhatsApp number, city/state, Instagram handle, prices, exact list of modalities, session durations, credentials.
- Legal/ethical: complementary therapy. Never promise cure, never "diagnóstico"/"tratamento"/"paciente" (use atendimento, sessão, cliente, harmonização). Must show a disclaimer that it does not replace medical/psychological care (Código Penal arts. 283/284; CDC arts. 36-37; CONAR).

## Brand Commitments

- Name as she signs it: "Anelise Werle Camillo — Terapeuta Quântica".
- Voice: warm, spiritual, uplifting, about light, positivity and helping; never clinical, never hype.
- Colors (user-pinned): emerald green and gold are the primary colors. Do not swap the ground for another hue.
- Feel (user-pinned): Apple-grade materials and motion, with generous, scroll-linked transitions.

## Evidence on Hand

- No photo, testimonials, prices, follower counts or press exist in the repo. Do not fabricate testimonials, numbers or credentials. Any illustrative quote must be labelled as an example to be replaced.

## Product Principles

1. One action: booking by WhatsApp is always one tap away.
2. Her words, not the category's: Dragões de Luz and Mesa Quântica lead; generic modality menus follow.
3. Honest about what it is: complementary, no cure claims, disclaimer visible.
4. Phone first: most visitors come from Instagram on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, readable body size for older visitors, reduced-motion respected, Portuguese (pt-BR) throughout.
